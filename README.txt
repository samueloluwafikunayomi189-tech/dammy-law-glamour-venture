DAMMY LAW GLAMOUR VENTURE — WEBSITE ZIP

Included:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- images/ (folder for your own image files)

SETUP:
1. Extract the ZIP on your phone.
2. Open script.js in Acode.
3. Replace 2340000000000 with your real WhatsApp business number (country code + number, digits only).
4. Open index.html in Chrome to preview.
5. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js and the images folder to your GitHub repository.

The sample catalogue uses online Unsplash image links, so internet access is needed for those photos. You can replace the sample product data and image links in script.js with your own products and photographs.

The cart supports add-to-bag, quantity changes, remove, subtotal calculation and WhatsApp checkout. It is a front-end starter: no database, admin dashboard, online card payment or stock management is included.
